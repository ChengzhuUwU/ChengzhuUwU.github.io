<h2 id="about">About</h2>
<p>LuisaComputeSimulator is a high-performance cross-platform physics simulator based on <a href="https://github.com/LuisaGroup/LuisaCompute">LuisaCompute</a>. It provides support for cloth and rigid-body simulations with penetration-free contact handling, accelerated by various GPU/CPU backends (e.g., CUDA, DirectX 12, Vulkan, Metal, and Fallback).</p>
